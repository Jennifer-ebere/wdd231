Master the Basics, Cook with Confidence 🌷

Preparation Tips

  1. Read the entire recipe before starting.
  2. Gather all ingredients before cooking (mise en place).
  3. Measure ingredients accurately.
  4. Wash fruits and vegetables thoroughly.
  5. Prep vegetables before turning on the stove.
  6. Keep your workspace clean and organized.
  7. Use separate cutting boards for meat and vegetables.
  8. Sharpen knives regularly for safer cutting.
  9. Pat meat dry before seasoning.
  10. Let frozen foods thaw completely before cooking.

Seasoning Tips

  1. Taste food as you cook.
  2. Season in layers rather than all at once.
  3. Add salt gradually.
  4. Fresh herbs provide brighter flavors than dried herbs.
  5. Use citrus juice to enhance flavors.
  6. A pinch of sugar can balance acidic dishes.
  7. Toast spices before using them for deeper flavor.
  8. Black pepper is best freshly ground.
  9. Don't over-season at the beginning.
  10. Add delicate herbs near the end of cooking.

Vegetable Cooking Tips

  1. Avoid overcrowding vegetables in the pan.
  2. Steam vegetables to preserve nutrients.
  3. Roast vegetables for a richer flavor.
  4. Add leafy greens at the end of cooking.
  5. Store potatoes in a cool, dark place.
  6. Soak cut potatoes before frying for crispier results.
  7. Blanch vegetables before freezing.
  8. Cut vegetables into similar sizes for even cooking.
  9. Use vegetable scraps to make broth.
  10. Don't overcook vegetables; they should retain some texture.

Frying Tips

  1. Heat oil before adding food.
  2. Don't overcrowd the frying pan.
  3. Drain fried foods on paper towels.
  4. Maintain consistent oil temperature.
  5. Use oils with high smoke points.
  6. Dry foods before frying.
  7. Test oil temperature with a small piece of food.
  8. Turn food carefully to avoid splattering.
  9. Replace old frying oil regularly.
  10. Allow fried foods to rest briefly before serving.

Time-Saving Tips

  1. Prep ingredients the night before.
  2. Cook extra portions for leftovers.
  3. Freeze chopped vegetables for future meals.
  4. Keep staple ingredients stocked.
  5. Use a slow cooker for busy days.
  6. Batch-cook soups and stews.
  7. Plan meals for the week.
  8. Store herbs in water to keep them fresh longer.
  9. Use pre-measured spice mixes.
  10. Clean as you cook.

General Cooking Wisdom

  1. Simpler recipes can be just as delicious.
  2. Practice makes cooking easier.
  3. Learn from mistakes instead of giving up.
  4. Fresh ingredients usually produce better results.
  5. Presentation matters.
  6. Balance sweet, salty, sour, bitter, and umami flavors.
  7. Trust your senses—look, smell, and taste.
  8. Keep experimenting with new ingredients.
  9. Every great cook started as a beginner.
  10. Cook with patience and enjoy the process.
