Tasty Bites Recipe Website - Site Plan

Jennifer C. Ebere

WDD 231

Site Name - Tasty Bites Recipes

This name was chosen because it reflects Nigerian-inspired food (“Tasty”) and delicious meals (“Bites”). It is catchy, easy to remember, and clearly communicates that the website focuses on food recipes.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide users with easy access to a variety of food recipes, cooking instructions, and meal ideas. The website will help users discover new meals, learn how to cook step-by-step recipes, and explore random meal suggestions using a food API.

Scenarios

  • What quick meals can I prepare with ingredients I already have at home?
  • Where can I find easy cooking instructions for beginner-friendly recipes?

#335c67 (Primary): Used for the website background, header sections, and large content areas.

#c1cfda (Secondary): Used for navigation bars, buttons, and hover effects.

#f26419 (Accent 1): Used for headings, recipe card highlights, and important accents.

#ffffff (Accent 2): Used for text backgrounds and card backgrounds to keep the design clean and readable.

Typography

  • Bellota: Used for headings and titles (H1, H2, H3).
  • Open Sans: Used for body text and paragraphs for readability.
