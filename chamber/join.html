NP Membership Benefits

The NP Membership is designed for non-profit organizations that want to participate in the chamber community at no cost.

  • Free membership with no annual fees
  • Access to selected community networking events
  • Participation in volunteer and outreach programs
  • Listing in the chamber directory
  • Access to basic business resources and announcements

This level is ideal for charities, NGOs, and community-focused organizations looking to build visibility and partnerships.

Bronze Membership Benefits

The Bronze Membership is an entry-level business package designed for small businesses and startups.

  • Access to monthly networking events
  • Basic listing in the business directory
  • Eligibility to attend training workshops
  • Limited promotional opportunities in newsletters
  • Access to business support resources

This level is perfect for new businesses seeking exposure and growth opportunities.

Silver Membership Benefits

The Silver Membership is designed for growing businesses that want stronger visibility and engagement opportunities.

  • All Bronze benefits included
  • Discounts on chamber-sponsored events
  • Featured placement in business directories
  • Access to business training and development programs
  • Advertising opportunities in newsletters and emails
  • Invitations to exclusive networking sessions

Ideal for businesses ready to expand their reach and customer base.

Gold Membership Benefits

The Gold Membership is the premium tier offering maximum visibility, promotion, and networking opportunities.

  • All Silver benefits included
  • Homepage spotlight advertising for your business
  • Priority listing in all directories
  • Exclusive VIP networking events with industry leaders
  • Free or heavily discounted event sponsorship opportunities
  • Premium advertising placements across chamber platforms
  • Early access to new programs and partnerships

This level is best suited for established businesses seeking strong brand recognition and influence.
